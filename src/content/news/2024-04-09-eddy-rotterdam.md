The first conference of the [European Network for Digital Democracy
(EDDY)](https://www.eddy-network.eu/in-person-events/eddy-2024-rotterdam),
"Digital Democracy: Bridging Theory and Practice", ran 9–11 April 2024 at
Erasmus University Rotterdam. I presented a poster there, "Partisan Bots or
Neutral Models? Politically Aligning Large Language Models", with Fynn
Bachmann, Cristina Sarasua and Abraham Bernstein.

The question behind it: can large language models be aligned to the viewpoints
of Swiss political parties? We had the models fill in a political questionnaire
and compared their ratings against those of actual Swiss politicians, testing
three routes to alignment — prompt engineering, retrieval augmented generation,
and finetuning.
