My first paper as a PhD student! Very happy to have collaborated with Fynn to
get this published in PLoS One.

We looked at how well GPT-4 can mimic user responses in a political survey, and
how such synthetic data helps to train statistical models in adaptive
questionnaires. This addresses the cold start problem, one of the main
limitations of an adaptive questionnaire. We applied the method to the Swiss
Voting Advice Application Smartvote, and showed that it can significantly
increase the recommendation accuracy for early users.
