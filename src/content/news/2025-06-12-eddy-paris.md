The second [EDDY conference](https://eddy-2025.sciencesconf.org/) ran 12–13
June 2025 at the Centre d'Économie de la Sorbonne in Paris. I presented
"AutoDQI: An Automated Measurement of Deliberation Quality" — an attempt to
score the quality of deliberation automatically, rather than by hand-coding
transcripts.

From a poster at the first conference, to an oral presentation at the second installment. Aiming for a keynote presentation at the next!
