A research report (Utrecht University / Data School) analysing nearly 50 million
messages on Twitter and Telegram to examine the dynamics between political debate
in the Dutch House of Representatives and the reactions, comments, and framing it
provokes on social media. A central focus is tracking how threatening and
radicalising language moves between parliamentary politics and online platforms.
